GROW YOUR HABITS, SQUARE BY SQUARE
Each habit is its own growing square. Validate a day, fill a cell. Watch the square expand as your consistency builds — a simple, satisfying visual you'll want to keep filling in.

KEEP YOUR TROPHIES FOREVER

UNLOCK BADGES FOR EVERY MILESTONE
20+ badges across streaks, totals, habit count, and time of day — plus a few secret ones to discover along the way. There's always a next milestone worth chasing.

CHOOSE YOUR OWN CHALLENGE LEVEL
Zen, Standard, or Hardcore — pick how forgiving Moto is when you miss a day. Go easy while you're building the habit, or turn up the pressure once it sticks.

BUILD GOOD HABITS, BREAK BAD ONES
Track habits you want to start (read more, drink water, meditate) and habits you want to quit (no phone after 10pm, no sugar) — Moto handles both the same simple way.

DAILY MOTIVATION, RIGHT WHEN YOU NEED IT
A fresh motivational quote greets you every day, plus smart reminder notifications that adapt to your time of day and progress — never generic, always relevant.

Whether you're building a morning routine, quitting a bad habit, training for consistency, or just trying to feel a little more in control of your days — Moto gives you a visual habit tracker, streak tracker, and daily routine companion that respects the days you have, not just the days you didn't.
